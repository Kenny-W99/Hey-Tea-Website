# HEYTEA Make Your Summer

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Make Your Summer"**

The three destination cups are the visitor’s map. Each sleeve identifies a drink at selection, travels in the mascot’s hand and appears again on the final postcard.

The working area pairs photographic fruit and an outlined white mascot with a quiet, warm-paper surface. Color and detail increase when the finished drink opens a city.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Destination-specific cup sleeves
- Photographic ingredients and illustrated helper poses
- Serif headlines beside plain-language controls

## Colors

Deep green ink anchors headlines and primary actions. A second green marks completed preparation steps. Muted green carries supporting copy, sage frames the screen, and warm paper carries reading and controls. A fine sage divider separates the wordmark and campaign title.

Guava pink identifies Miami’s Guava Sunset, gold identifies Hawaii’s Golden Aloha and peach identifies LA’s Desert Glow. The selected recipe supplies the backing color for its cup and a lighter wash for its station.

## Typography

DM Serif Display carries the large campaign and task headings, with italic emphasis on short phrases. Kalam is reserved for the handwritten campaign line on the poster. DM Sans carries ingredient names, instructions, control labels and status. Instruction paragraphs have a maximum width of 38 characters.

## Layout

At desktop sizes, the entry poster shares a single rounded frame with the invitation. The station pairs instructions with a larger work area. Travel gives the landmark the full scene width and places controls below it. The shell is capped at 1500px.

At 820px and below, the split layout tightens. At 650px and below, the entry poster sits above the camera choices, and the station art sits above its instruction. The three selection buttons sit under one large image of the selected cup.

## Elevation & Depth

The poster lifts slightly off its sage surround. Shadows beneath the printed cups keep overlapping silhouettes readable. City foreground plants can pass in front of the walking mascot. Keep the sleeve and its lettering oriented correctly in both directions.

## Shapes

Rounded scene edges contain the art. Cup backings have high arched tops and shallow lower corners. Actions use pill outlines. The selected phone destination has an inset green outline and an underlined place name. Keyboard focus uses a separate outer rust ring.

## Components

- **Camera invitation.** Stack the filled primary action over the outlined direct-controls action. Keep status and recovery copy beside those controls.
- **Drink choice.** Show destination and drink names on each choice. Repeat the current drink name above its ingredients and confirmation button.
- **Preparation station.** Pair one pictured action with a short instruction, a segmented step indicator and a text percentage. The stirring illustration pairs the spoon with a dotted circular path.
- **Travel and postcard.** Place the stop title above the landscape. Keep the carried cup specific to the selected drink. The final postcard sets the larger cup beside the mascot and returns to the same destination.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the recipe name, printed sleeve and illustrated place together.
- Do keep action text on a readable surface and preserve the visible keyboard focus ring.
- Don't mirror lettering on a cup when reversing a travel pose.
- Don't use the shaker illustration for the circular stirring action.
