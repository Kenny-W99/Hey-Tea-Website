The choice to show continuous input feedback draws on Microsoft’s hand-interaction guidance. The neutral-position joystick mapping is a project design choice for this browser experience. [Hand interaction and visible feedback](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/mixed-reality/design/direct-manipulation)

The revised follow view was checked at all twelve landings. The lighthouse approach has a dedicated foot offset to keep the traveler inside the rail, and vertical framing preserves the lighthouse lantern. The video shows the application screen while the actual browser recognizer tracks animated photographic hand inputs. The webcam image itself is absent from the capture.

## Physical usability evidence still needed

Screenshot sign-off covers composition, artwork and visible cues in the captured states. The plan’s camera acceptance criteria call for full recipes and city travel with real hands on supported devices, including varied lighting, hand loss and recovery. The first-time-user target is four of five people finishing a camera recipe with the on-screen cues alone. Those observations, measured latency, thermal behavior and sustained performance require separate records from hands-on testing. [Acceptance criteria, §17](../plan.md#17-acceptance-criteria)
